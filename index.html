JobDashboard

A personal job-search dashboard that runs on its owner's own computer.

JobDashboard collects job postings from public job boards, scores them against a resume, and tracks applications from saved to offer. It is a single-person tool: it runs locally, and it is not offered as a service to the public.

Google sign-in

The owner can connect two Google features, each with its own consent:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): finds emails about applications already being tracked, such as "application received", interview invites and rejections, and suggests a status change. Nothing changes until the owner clicks to apply it. The app never sends, deletes, archives, labels or marks email as read.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): saves generated cover letters and resumes as Google Docs. The app can only see the files it created.

See the privacy policy for what is read, what is kept, and how to remove it.
